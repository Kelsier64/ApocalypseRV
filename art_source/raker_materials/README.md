# Raker skin material source

2026-10-25. `dry_skin_albedo_source.png` is an original synthetic skin sample made with the built-in ImageGen tool, not a photograph or a scanned material. It is the color/detail source for the current Raker skin and hand atlases.

Blender 5.2.3 LTS projects the sample in object space with blended box projection, desaturates it, adds restrained joint/eye/scar coloration, and bakes it onto the existing UVs. Fine sample luminance contributes shallow bump detail; the earlier anatomical normal map is retained at reduced strength. Keratin is authored separately with longitudinal grain and greater roughness than the first preview. Runtime uses ordinary baked glTF PBR textures; no procedural shader or added geometry is required.

Final asset: [raker.glb](../../assets/models/raker/raker.glb). Skin albedo/normal are 4096², hands 2048², nails 1024²; ORM is 1024² for skin and 512² for hands/nails. Texture import settings retain mipmaps and high-quality VRAM compression. Authoring scripts, intermediate maps and review renders remain in the ignored `.godot/art-work/raker-material-realism/` working directory.

## ImageGen prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless tileable physically based 3D creature skin BASE COLOR / ALBEDO texture, square 2048 x 2048. Create an extremely convincing macrophotographic skin material sample, like a professionally scanned PBR texture of the dry leathery skin of an old pale humanoid horror creature. Fill the entire canvas edge to edge with skin surface, no body parts, no background. Restrained desaturated warm gray beige with thin translucent muted pink-gray and faint purplish capillaries beneath the skin. Actual fine pores, irregular tiny polygonal skin microfurrows, fine overlapping wrinkles with natural directions, a few tiny age spots and worn dry flakes. Sparse fine broken capillaries, not tree branches. Subtle biologically organized variation rather than clouds, granite, concrete, dirt camouflage, bark, reptile scales, leather upholstery or fabric. Skin should be realistic delicate tissue with finer grain, not thick cracked earth. Very fine lines and pores dominate. Macro texture sample covers approximately 25 centimeters of skin in the real world, seen straight-on. Uniform flat diffuse cross-polarized illumination: NO cast shadows, NO highlights, NO directional gradient, NO ambient occlusion painted into deep creases, NO vignette, NO depth of field. No large scars, no blood, no holes, no exposed tissue, no hair, no text, no label, no border. Make borders seamless and tileable in both axes. This is a final material texture for a realistic 3D game character, not a concept illustration.
