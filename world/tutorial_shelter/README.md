# Tutorial shelter assets and course controllers

New v12 runs use `orientation_controller.gd` for the 20 m reception and 40 m workshop. `orientation_workshop.gd` records committed equipment, recycling, gasoline, wall/tire and refueling transactions. `orientation_recovery.gd` owns independent full-world entrances for reception and each workshop step. Retry is staged and transactional; snapshots persist with the current save without nesting or overwriting the manual file. Esc can skip even if the original terminal is lost, without granting stock or fuel. The original terminal becomes a searchable manual in every ownership form, preserving portable v2 boot and power fields.

The original RV moving forward inside the advance sensor opens the exit while simulation remains in preparation. The player and every RV collision, including protruding mounted devices, must clear the exit while the vehicle is ready before time, weather and enemies resume. The physical gate reopens on obstruction, then seals permanently after clearance. Course v3 accepts v1/v2 saves; v11 keeps the older independent controller. See the [two-room stage-three validation](../../docs/validation/2026-10-15-orientation-stage3.md). Final two-room art remains stage four; the following sections describe retained v11 assets.

Stage-one v11 layout: nine bays, 40 × 416 m interior (local Z −402 to 14 m). Bay lengths are 80, 40, 40, 48, 40, 40, 40, 40 and 48 m; inner partitions are at local Z −322, −282, −242, −194, −154, −114, −74 and −34 m. The exit stays fixed and RVSpawn moves to local Z −388 m. The old v7–v10 shelter is unchanged.

- `bay_gate.tscn` reuses the original shelter's leaf geometry, handles, warning strips and materials. Leaf collision remains 3.5 × 5 × 0.3 m. Its track is 0.85 m in front of the partition so opened leaves clear the complete wall finish and frame. The warning stripe sits ahead of the raised brace. Jambs, header and lintel meet at their edges instead of overlapping.
- `gate_button_model.tscn` reuses the original physical case, warning plate, button and lamp. `bay_gate_button.gd` inherits the original press/color animation; each instance owns its animated materials. Outside controls rotate the complete model toward the approaching player.
- `wall_surface.tscn` reuses a clipped 8 m section of the old `starting_shelter/facade.tscn` interior cast-wall skin, runoff, fracture and repair surfaces. Vertex wear colors and existing shader/texture references are preserved. Height adapts from 6.8 to 8.8 m; world-space texture mapping keeps texel density. `.res` meshes and `.tres` materials are the delivered reusable modules, without runtime mesh generation. Each module is 630 triangles. Long outer wall skins use 12 m spans by scaling this shared 8 m mesh by 1.5; world-space texture mapping retains texture density while limiting instance count.
- `industrial_light.tscn` is a shared enclosed lamp with a diffuser, end caps and guard. Each interior instance is connected to the roof by two anchored suspension rods and clamps; the porch instance uses a wall bracket. Paired interior fixtures are spaced roughly 20 m apart, with four pairs in Bay01 and two pairs in each other bay. Interior light energy is 1.25 (previously 2.4), porch energy 1.0 (previously 2.0), and diffuser emission 0.45 (previously 1.2).
- Large structural BoxMeshes have edges subdivided to at most 8 m to prevent directional shadow pancaking from clipping the huge roof triangles. Physical collision shapes are unchanged. Indoor fixtures cast shadows so light cannot pass through partitions and closed gates.

The 2026-10-13 repair measured 162,522 static mesh instance triangles before the bay extension. The extended layout remains subject to the 200,000 triangle budget; its current count has not been measured. Source/intermediate extraction tools and render evidence stay in ignored `.godot/`. Full decorative treatment and performance tuning remain stage six.

The tutorial uses `first_briefing.tscn`, the powered `TrainingTerminal` Item, and `tutorial_controller.gd` for progress and retry snapshots. `tutorial_workshop.gd` owns lessons 2–6, one-time supply setup, designated IDs, and successful-transaction evidence. Lesson 2 uses the designated spare battery; lesson 3 requires viewing `rvctl` Overview, Energy and Equipment and successfully switching the functional cabin strip off and on. Lesson 4 requires fastening both new training machines to the original RV, cancelling one preview, and removing/reinstalling either unit. Lesson 5 recycles two designated scraps and an oil barrel through the training scrapper; their yields total 8 Metal Parts and 10 Unrefined Fuel. Lesson 6 requires actual gasoline output pickup from the training workbench, identified by persistent item ID. See the [stage-three validation record](../../docs/validation/2026-10-13-tutorial-stage3.md) for scenarios, logs and the pending hands-on acceptance walkthrough.

The nine-bay extension adds 20 m to every bay. At the user's request, no tests, checks or gameplay validation were run for that extension; previous stage-one evidence describes the earlier layout. Phase three adds ordinary Items at stable shelter-local positions. Its battery objective uses the original RV socket and excludes reinstalling the original battery. Fresh fuel is 40; preparation floors of 10 fuel / 20 valid installed battery charge do not count as transactions. The extended static-mesh triangle count has not been measured against the 200,000 budget.

`tutorial_service.gd` owns lessons 7–9. Bay 7 configures its faults once after full parking, shutdown, handbrake and seat exit, then captures the stable retry boundary. Actual Structure repair, original-engine kit repair and original-wheel removal/marked-spare installation are independent conditions that also require current usability. Bay 8 transfers the manufactured can's fuel and returns a real empty can. Bay 9 accepts the departure button only with a ready RV and closed maintenance cover; the physical exit reopens on obstruction and seals after the player and entire RV clear it. The same terminal then becomes a searchable field manual. Esc Skip records `skipped`, opens all inner gates, and unlocks the terminal in every existing ownership form without creating a replacement. Formal departure ends preparation floors and disables tutorial retry/skip. See [phase four](../../docs/validation/2026-10-14-tutorial-stage4.md) and [phase five](../../docs/validation/2026-10-14-tutorial-stage5.md) for evidence and acceptance.
