# Tutorial shelter assets

Stage-one v11 layout: nine bays, 40 × 416 m interior (local Z −402 to 14 m). Bay lengths are 80, 40, 40, 48, 40, 40, 40, 40 and 48 m; inner partitions are at local Z −322, −282, −242, −194, −154, −114, −74 and −34 m. The exit stays fixed and RVSpawn moves to local Z −388 m. The old v7–v10 shelter is unchanged.

- `bay_gate.tscn` reuses the original shelter's leaf geometry, handles, warning strips and materials. Leaf collision remains 3.5 × 5 × 0.3 m. Its track is 0.85 m in front of the partition so opened leaves clear the complete wall finish and frame. The warning stripe sits ahead of the raised brace. Jambs, header and lintel meet at their edges instead of overlapping.
- `gate_button_model.tscn` reuses the original physical case, warning plate, button and lamp. `bay_gate_button.gd` inherits the original press/color animation; each instance owns its animated materials. Outside controls rotate the complete model toward the approaching player.
- `wall_surface.tscn` reuses a clipped 8 m section of the old `starting_shelter/facade.tscn` interior cast-wall skin, runoff, fracture and repair surfaces. Vertex wear colors and existing shader/texture references are preserved. Height adapts from 6.8 to 8.8 m; world-space texture mapping keeps texel density. `.res` meshes and `.tres` materials are the delivered reusable modules, without runtime mesh generation. Each module is 630 triangles. Long outer wall skins use 12 m spans by scaling this shared 8 m mesh by 1.5; world-space texture mapping retains texture density while limiting instance count.
- `industrial_light.tscn` is a shared enclosed lamp with a diffuser, end caps and guard. Each interior instance is connected to the roof by two anchored suspension rods and clamps; the porch instance uses a wall bracket. Paired interior fixtures are spaced roughly 20 m apart, with four pairs in Bay01 and two pairs in each other bay. Interior light energy is 1.25 (previously 2.4), porch energy 1.0 (previously 2.0), and diffuser emission 0.45 (previously 1.2).
- Large structural BoxMeshes have edges subdivided to at most 8 m to prevent directional shadow pancaking from clipping the huge roof triangles. Physical collision shapes are unchanged. Indoor fixtures cast shadows so light cannot pass through partitions and closed gates.

The 2026-10-13 repair measured 162,522 static mesh instance triangles before the bay extension. The extended layout remains subject to the 200,000 triangle budget; its current count has not been measured. Source/intermediate extraction tools and render evidence stay in ignored `.godot/`. Full decorative treatment and performance tuning remain stage six.

The nine-bay extension adds 20 m to every bay. At the user's request, no tests, checks or gameplay validation were run for this extension; the user will inspect it personally. Previous validation evidence describes the earlier layout.
