# Starting shelter art

`starting_shelter.tscn` is an authored, continuous-world military motor pool. Units are metres, the floor is Y=0, and local +Z points out through the garage gate. The production site rotates the frontage toward the highway; the RV itself retains its original -Z forward convention.

- Design dimensions: 20 x 28 m interior, roof beams above 6 m, 7 x 5 m clear vehicle doorway, and a 32 x 30 m forecourt from Z=14 to Z=44. The expanded decorative building is 50 x 45 m and 14 m above ground: X=-25..25, Z=-30.5..14.5, Y=-0.4..14. The slab sits below the floor. Terrain must sit just below the slab to avoid coincident surfaces.
- `ExteriorExtension` adds solid, inaccessible west/east wings (9/11 m roof heights), a 12 m rear block and rooftop plant placeholders reaching 14 m. `Visuals` is replaceable independently of its sibling `Collision`. The building itself is finished native scene geometry: aged concrete walls, broken roof coping, facade piers, rusted sealed steel window panels and faded military markings in `facade.tscn`. The air handler is modeled at the front edge of the west-wing roof, local center (-17.7,10,9.8), visible from the forecourt at player eye height. Its collider and concrete plinth follow the visual; the navigation marker remains. The filter bank sits at the front edge of the east-wing roof, local center (17.7,11.75,9.8), visible from the forecourt at player eye height. Its box collision, hidden graybox and concrete plinth follow the visual; its navigation marker remains. See the [air handler request](../../docs/modeling/requests/shelter-roof-air-handler/shelter-roof-air-handler.md) and [filter bank request](../../docs/modeling/requests/shelter-roof-filter-bank/shelter-roof-filter-bank.md). There is no whole-building modeling request. Original garage interior layout, gate motion/collision, supplies, gameplay markers and apron geometry are unchanged; surface materials and decorative lettering have been rebuilt. The POI's shared site bounds are X=-27..27, Z=-33..46, Y=-0.5..14.5; v7 bank shaping follows building bounds. Content/save identity remains unchanged; v2-v6 assets are unaffected.
- `Visuals`, `Collision`, `Furnishings`, and `Lights` own static presentation. `GarageGate/LeftLeaf` and `RightLeaf` are separate moving bodies. `GarageButton` is a separate interaction body. The run controller attaches their behavior; this scene does not create actors or own run state.
- `RVSpawn`, `PlayerSpawn`, `ExteriorExit`, `AccessPoints/Front`, and `AccessPoints/Apron` are stable integration markers. `StarterEquipment` contains Generator, CraftingStation, and Scrapper; `StarterLoot` contains Flashlight, Battery, GasCan1/2, Scrap1..6, OilBarrel, and Wheel. Markers include each item's required origin height.
- Extension box shapes carry `navigation_solid` metadata. Chunk baking projects their full volume as obstructions (including neighbour-source reconstruction), removing terrain navigation inside the sealed blocks. Keep this metadata and the independent collision shapes when replacing the exterior art.
- The central lane and rear ramp zone remain clear. Optional equipment sits in three marked bays on the player's starting side of the vehicle; supplies sit on the floor opposite, within carrying distance of both vehicle entrances.

The facade uses actual 45 cm exterior wall segments and recessed, permanently closed louver windows, bevelled pillars/sills/lintels, roof slabs, parapets and caps. `facade.tscn` now also owns the replacement 40 cm garage side/rear walls, 30 cm entrance cheeks/lintel and bevelled interior piers. Their former simple visual meshes are hidden; every original collision volume and gameplay node remains. The authored asset contains 17,604 triangles in eleven material batches, including the interior surfaces. These are static meshes, not runtime tessellation.

Wall damage is recessed into the actual concrete surface, up to approximately 7 cm deep, with coarse planar fracture faces; it never opens an explorable hole. Narrow 6 mm cold joints follow 2.2 m pouring lifts. Vertex channels encode pour variation, exposed aggregate and joints. Most wall areas remain flat; only local damage gets a 12 cm authoring grid. Original solid collision envelopes are intentionally retained over these shallow surface losses. Window returns, garage clearances and inaccessible wings keep their previous dimensions.

Damage silhouettes use individually authored concave polygons, not radial masks. Selected bays have narrow vertical losses beside piers, broken strips along cold joints, small scrapes, sill-corner losses or isolated base chips. Opposite walls and entrance cheeks have different distributions, and several bays are intact. Noise roughens only the polygon edges. See the [damage distribution correction](../../docs/validation/2026-10-03-shelter-spall-distribution.md).

`materials/formed_concrete.gdshader` uses neutral grey/taupe cement, restrained mortar texture, aggregate in damaged areas, small tie holes and damp bases. Grain fades with screen footprint; it is not a full-wall procedural camouflage pattern. Authored runoff starts below selected sills, damaged coping and service pipes. Stress cracks start at window corners; small flush mortar repairs and actual parapet chips add distinct maintenance/wear history. Old paint bands and floating spall patches are not used. Service pipes use simple eight-sided cylinders.

`materials/aged_metal.gdshader` supplies brown oxidation on charcoal steel for the doors, structural steel and sealed louvers. Its object-space coordinates keep the rust attached to the moving door leaves. The starting site's floor material is warm dusty grey; other bunker material resources are untouched. All weathering is static presentation; it creates no loot, saves, runtime generation or collision nodes.

The 2026-10-03 finish pass lowers concrete reflectance and specular response, adds fine pitting/aggregate bump, and deepens irregular damp/grime and localized runoff. Additional spalling sits below sills, service pipes and at the wall base. The four garage work lights use energy 1.65 (previously 2.2); the entrance lamp and world lighting remain unchanged. See [the darker wall validation](../../docs/validation/2026-10-03-shelter-dark-walls.md).

The previous shader-only style refinement is recorded in [the wall style validation](../../docs/validation/2026-10-03-shelter-wall-style.md). The subsequent structural rebuild is recorded in [the cast-wall rebuild](../../docs/validation/2026-10-03-shelter-wall-rebuild.md). It follows the GDD's cold grey/taupe industrial low-poly direction and keeps the approved aged door material and dim work lighting. Cement texture comes from the existing POI kit; exposed aggregate reuses [the independent wall study texture](../../assets/materials/ruined_wall/README.md) with restrained contrast. The study's high-density mesh, through-hole and rebar were not imported into the production shelter.

Offline authoring source: [build_shelter_walls.gd](../../scripts/build_shelter_walls.gd). It writes only `facade.tscn` and requires explicit `-- --write`; ordinary gameplay never generates or writes the meshes. Run `godot --headless --path . -s res://scripts/build_shelter_walls.gd -- --write` to intentionally regenerate it. Any manual edits to that generated facade should be moved into its source before regenerating. The parent scene, collisions, machinery placeholders, and functional nodes are maintained separately.

`roadblock.tscn` has its root at the road centre, with local X across the road and the approach on -Z. It reuses 28 closely interlocked static wrecks in two layers, including four overturned upper cars. Original car-body colliders overlap laterally across X approximately -26..26; the visible pile is about 3.5 m high. A 1.05 m rubble backing sits behind the wrecks, with no invisible collision above it. Retaining wings extend to X=+/-452 and Y=12 m, continuing into the terrain ridge so the changed central silhouette does not open a route around the road shoulders. Production wheel-driven centre and shoulder approaches are covered by `tests/test_starting_roadblock.gd`; rerun it after changing the pile. Terrain integration and the placement at world Z=20 are owned by the world generator.

All new shell, gate, floor, markings, and barrier geometry is native Godot mesh-and-material work. Existing bunker v2 materials and simple furniture are reused without changing their shared resources. Their concrete and industrial texture provenance remains in [the concrete README](../../assets/materials/poi_kit/README.md) and [industrial materials README](../../assets/materials/industrial/README.md).
