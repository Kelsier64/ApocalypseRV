# Abandoned service fan

Static ground-level megashelter fitting, 3.1 × 2.8 × 2.4 m; +Y up, +Z intake/front, origin at bottom centre. The production wrapper places it at y=0.32 m on a concrete plinth. The adjoining duct, wall collar and collision envelope are authored in `MegashelterDetails`.

13,039 triangles; shared GLB and imported textures. Generated through ComfyUI and reduced with gltfpack; source, provenance and remaining topology limitations: [source notes](../../../art_source/shelter_service_fan/README.md). No gameplay interaction or animation.
