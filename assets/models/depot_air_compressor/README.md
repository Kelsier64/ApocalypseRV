# Depot air compressor

ComfyUI / TRELLIS.2 static prop made from the user-approved original reference. Runtime GLB has 4,990 triangles, one mesh/material and three PBR maps; no animation, physics or interaction scripts. Godot-extracted textures remain beside the GLB.

Bounds are 1.45 × 0.95 × 0.65 m, bottom-centre origin, +Y up, service side −Z. The workshop instances it at `(-16.8,0.03,12.95)`, with a separate authored box collision outside the search aisles. Tank, motor, gauge and handle are static approximations; reduction facets wheel hubs and small fins.
