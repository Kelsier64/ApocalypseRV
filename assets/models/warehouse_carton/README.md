# Sealed warehouse carton

Original editable 44-triangle bevelled OBJ, 1 × 0.6 × 0.7 m, centred origin, +Y up. Replaces the 96 decorative equipment-box instances in the v12 warehouse while preserving their transforms and authored collision envelopes.

`world/orientation_shelter/sealed_carton.tscn` applies an original procedural kraft-paper material with packing tape, a closure seam and a paper shipping label. All surface details are shaded on the same closed mesh; no overlapping decal planes. There are no handles, equipment latches, Item scripts, storage interaction or embedded collision. Actual collectible equipment keeps its existing model and behavior.

Budget: 100 triangles per carton; final 44. Source geometry remains directly editable in the OBJ, and the paper material is in `world/orientation_shelter/materials/kraft_carton.gdshader`.
