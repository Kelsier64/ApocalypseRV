# White apparition

Authored in Blender 5.2; exported as a 2.2 m, Y-up skinned glTF. Feet are at the origin and the face points along -Z. A single welded anatomical surface is bound to 17 bones, with continuous weighted deformation across shoulders, elbows, knees and spine. Bone names are consumed by `enemies/white_apparition/white_apparition_visual.gd`; preserve their names when replacing the mesh.

Faceless tapered anatomy, narrow joints, elongated fingers, no clothing or text. Cross-section lofts are welded and smoothed before skinning; there are no separate spherical joint pieces. Anatomical weight masks isolate the chest from arms and hands from legs, with smooth blending at actual joints. Detached fingertip voxel fragments are removed. Godot replaces the authoring material with a soft-edge pale shader and two faint, closely delayed pose echoes. Three large motion phrases combine serpentine side folding, opposing pelvis/chest torsion and asymmetric arm sweeps with alternating knee lifts. Short freezes and sharp shudders interrupt the dance. The lowest sole determines vertical placement; horizontal foot sliding can still occur. Collision and encounter logic belong to the scene wrapper, not this asset.

Editable raw source and generation/review intermediates remain locally in the ignored `.godot/white_apparition/` directory (`source.blend`, `build_continuous.py`); only the runtime GLB is delivered. The model is authored for this project, with no external asset dependencies.

Validation (2026-11-09): inspected neutral front/side and three deformed poses with an opaque material in Godot, plus 12 seconds of rendered motion using the runtime material. Exported GLB has one connected surface; chest/waist weights are anatomically constrained, and all 2,222 hand/thumb vertices have no influence outside their own arm chain. The focused white-apparition behavior test passed. Local preview outputs are `preview/white_apparition_review.png` and `preview/white_apparition_motion.gif` under the ignored directory above. These are isolated model previews, not a manual main-world playthrough.
