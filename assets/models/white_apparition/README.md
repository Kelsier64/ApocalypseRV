# White apparition

Authored in Blender 5.2; exported as a 2.64 m, Y-up skinned glTF, facing -Z. The mesh's lowest point is 0.03003 m above the origin; the visual controller lowers it by that amount. A single welded surface is bound to 30 bones: hips, eight spine segments, two neck segments, head, five segments per arm and four per leg. Bone names are consumed by `enemies/white_apparition/white_apparition_visual.gd`; preserve their names when replacing the mesh.

The slender faceless silhouette has narrow shoulders, an understated waist, smooth limbs and simplified tapered hands. It has no muscles, exposed joints, clothing or text. Cross-section lofts are welded and smoothed before skinning. Anatomical masks isolate hands from legs and blend arms into the shoulders. Godot supplies a nearly plain soft-edge white shader with faint pose echoes delayed by 40/80 ms. Traveling bends pass through the spine and delayed neck; independent arm waves form long arcs. Five motion phrases blend continuously: traveling waves, opposed waist/chest torsion, forward arm weaving, a side fold with one arm raised, and a suspended gesture followed by a faster release. Phrase duration and pace vary, consecutive sustained phrases differ, and brief shudders occasionally interrupt them. Two-link leg IK keeps the ankles planted and feet level relative to the actor while its hips shift. The actor's existing lateral drift still moves the whole apparition; this is not terrain-adaptive foot placement. Spawn clearance is 2.75 m high. Encounter logic belongs to the wrapper.

Editable raw source and generation/review intermediates remain locally in the ignored `.godot/white_apparition/` directory (`source.blend`, `build_continuous.py`); only the runtime GLB is delivered. The model is authored for this project, with no external asset dependencies.
