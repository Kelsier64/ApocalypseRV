# Megashelter surface textures

Original albedo images generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-28, copied unmodified into the repository. These are authored game textures, not calibrated material scans. The originals remain in the local generated-images library.

- `cast_concrete_albedo.png`: pale cast concrete with fine pores and sand grains. Source `exec-6ad09d0f-8e65-4faa-aad5-ab0210d299fe.png`.
- `aged_coating_albedo.png`: neutral matte enamel, sparse chipped paint and oxide, suitable for tinting. Source `exec-bcd16eae-49bf-4d85-9449-62084a540a19.png`.
- `decayed_facade_albedo.png`: extensive flaking gray-green mineral paint, rough exposed cement and embedded mildew. Source `exec-6f505a0a-59e8-4c0d-9d04-ad1af8f35246.png`.
- `corroded_steel_albedo.png`: large connected areas of dark oxide, rust scales and remaining charcoal paint. Source `exec-87571762-bfc8-4b65-ab43-7575c442047d.png`.

Source images are 1254 × 1254. Godot imports the two base textures at up to 1024 pixels and the two decay textures at up to **512 pixels**, with high-quality VRAM compression and mipmaps. Nominal primary coverage is 2 × 2 object-space metres for base grain, 6 × 6 m for facade peeling and 4 × 4 m for steel corrosion. Each module and 4.5 m panel row has two independently offset and quarter-turned sampling domains, with a primary mirror, scale factors of 0.68–1.42 / 0.55–1.35 and continuous noise warping. Peeling and corrosion each blend two differently sized samples through an irregular spatial mask. Explicit texture gradients include the rotation, scale and continuous warp, while excluding discrete panel changes from mip selection. Module dimensions do not stretch the texture. Large peeled areas, dark runoff and brown rust must remain readable through the retro filter and reduced render scale. High-resolution grain is only supplementary.

Consumer: [megashelter surface shader](../../../world/megashelter/megashelter_surface.gdshader). Concrete, decayed cassettes and corroded steel have separate roughness/specular/metallic responses. Independent seeded values vary aggregate exposure, remaining paint, oxide depth and restrained warm/cool tint. Shallow bump comes from image intensity and is artistic relief, not a measured normal map. Exposed spalls reuse the existing [aggregate texture](../ruined_wall/README.md), selected by vertex alpha; alpha does not control transparency. UV2 stores a stable module seed and its real runoff ledge height; flat interpolation keeps hashing stable. Object-space weathering/bleaching varies over tens of metres and crosses panel boundaries independently of the local texture. Rain starts at wall tops or beam/lintel undersides; rust-water trails follow up to four authored drain lines, prioritising the entrance pair. These masks stay upright in building space and never inherit rotated/mirrored/warped texture coordinates. Damp deposits darken the foot and horizontal slabs collect irregular dirt. The kit retains exposed rebar, missing vent slats, sealed backing and existing collision. All changes share the original three material batches and existing bitmap assets. The panel and steel branches each add one texture lookup for their second sample; concrete does not use that extra sample.

Generation specifications: seamless square base-color tiles, flat orthographic material coverage, uniform diffuse illumination, no baked directional light, borders, panel grids, fasteners, lettering, objects or preview spheres. Concrete: neutral warm light gray, dense cement, extremely fine pores, restrained mineral mottling, no coarse pebble aggregate, large fractures or camouflage patches. Coating: neutral gray baked enamel on steel, 90% intact paint, sparse narrow chips exposing graphite steel and dark umber oxide, no large black/orange islands. Both requested approximately 2 m physical coverage and seamless edges; repetition is softened in the shader by low-contrast object-space mineral and runoff variation.

Decay additions requested decades of abandonment: facade paint missing across roughly 40% of the tile, large connected flakes, embedded mildew and fissures; steel with roughly 45% charcoal paint remaining among connected dark chocolate/burnt-sienna corrosion. These use the same flat albedo/no-text/no-grid constraints. Their larger runtime scale is intentional for low-resolution viewing.
