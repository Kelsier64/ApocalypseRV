# Player crouch

Two Godot AnimationLibrary variants share the existing v020 player skeleton. Each contains `idle` (2 s) and `forward`, `back`, `left`, `right` (0.6 s each), baked at 60 Hz. All tracks target `PLAYER_Rig/Skeleton3D` bones; the actor has no root motion.

`crouch.res` preserves the original deep squat, including the relaxed arm correction. `crouch_bent.res` is the new default: the hips sit over the feet, knees advance over the toes, and both shins lean forward about 39 degrees in idle. The waist bends forward while the head stays raised to look ahead; arms hang with bent elbows and relaxed wrists. During movement the trailing heel rolls up onto the forefoot instead of locking the boot flat. Both variants have a small alternating arm swing.

Directional cycles alternate support over 60% of each cycle. The bent variant uses a shorter 34 cm planted-foot travel, stored as `planted_foot_travel` library metadata; the preserved original uses 42 cm. The runtime matches playback to actual movement speed. Foot targets were corrected against evaluated boot skin; rest transforms, weights, meshes and triangle counts are unchanged. These are native bone-animation assets and require no duplicate character model or texture. Once the actor is ready, assign either library to `Visuals/Locomotion.crouch_clips` to choose a variant; input, collision, camera and carry behavior are shared. Standing locomotion fills omitted constant bone channels from its source rig so the neck and head blend back completely after either crouch.

Validate both variants' geometry with `test_player_animation_skin`, default behavior with `test_player_crouch`, and item grips with `test_player_carry`. Visual review: `godot --path . --log-file .godot/crouch-review.log res://tests/player_animation_playground.tscn -- --crouch`; F3 plays the four directions, first-person view and low tunnel. Replace `--crouch` with `--crouch-relaxed` to compare the preserved original. Captures go to ignored `.godot/crouch-bent-review/` and `.godot/crouch-relaxed-review/` respectively.
